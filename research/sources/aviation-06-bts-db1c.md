# Origin and Destination Survey Data

Thursday, April 30, 2026

**The Origin and Destination Survey (O&D DB1B & O&D DB1C)**

The Origin and Destination Survey (O&D DB1B & O&D DB1C) is a sample of airline tickets collected from reporting carriers. The data includes details for passengers transported, such as origin, destination, ticket price, and other itinerary information.

**Key Facts About Data Collection**

- **O&D DB1B:** Prior to July 2025, data was collected quarterly at a 10% sample rate. Collection is no longer active as of July 2025.
- **O&D DB1C:** Effective July 2025, data is collected monthly at a 40% sample rate.

**Additional Formats and Record Description**

- **Transtats Homepage:** You can visit the [Bureau of Transportation Statistics Transtats homepage](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/) for additional O&D DB1B formats.
- **Record Description:** You can also view the [Record Description for the O&D DB1C Data Sets](https://www.bts.gov/explore-topics-and-geography/data-bank-1c-public-version).
