# County Business Patterns Data

[County Business Patterns (CBP)](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp.html) has been produced as a consistent, annual series since 1964 and ZIP Code Business Patterns (ZBP) since 1994.

The most current CBP data are for reference year 2023. We do not have a planned release date for our 2024 data at this time. The Census Bureau is evaluating alternative approaches to comply with the U.S. Department of Commerce’s [administrative order](https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.commerce.gov%2Fopog%2Fdisclosure-avoidance-statistical-products&data=05%7C02%7Cmelissa.kopajtic%40census.gov%7C43aea289692f413f170308df13168491%7C3aa716f1e55941cea53047d18313c603%7C0%7C0%7C639250659634824729%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=CkZqsLl%2FMO93e1YxnZiadGSmexIslWu4jRWz2HoWWzo%3D&reserved=0 "Follow link") prohibiting the use of noise infusion. Updated guidance will be provided once plans are finalized.
